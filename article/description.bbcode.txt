[size=5][b]Quick Product Manager – Faster Product & Category Management for OpenCart 3[/b][/size]
Manage your OpenCart products and categories faster from a single, streamlined interface. Edit products without navigating between pages, update multiple items at once, and access essential catalog information without the limitations of the default administration lists.

[b]Quick Product Manager[/b] replaces the standard product and category lists with an AJAX-powered management interface, featuring inline controls, bulk actions, advanced filters, and a convenient side-drawer editor.

Spend less time managing your catalog and more time growing your store.

[size=4][b]Why Quick Product Manager?[/b][/size]

Managing a growing product catalog through the default OpenCart interface can be time-consuming.
[list]
[*][b]Too many page reloads:[/b] Every product edit requires navigating away from the list and returning afterward.
[*][b]Repetitive updates:[/b] Updating multiple products means performing the same actions individually.
[*][b]Limited product filtering:[/b] Finding products by SKU, manufacturer, category, or other identifiers takes extra steps.
[*][b]Fragmented category management:[/b] Important category settings are spread across different fields and screens.
[/list]

Quick Product Manager brings these workflows together in one place.

[size=4][b]Key Benefits[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]One-screen management:[/b] Browse products, apply filters, and edit details without leaving the management page.
[*][b]Convenient side-drawer editor:[/b] Open any product or category directly from its list.
[*][b]Powerful bulk actions:[/b] Update product status, categories, manufacturers, and more in just a few clicks.
[*][b]Advanced filtering:[/b] Find products using multiple identifiers and catalog attributes.
[*][b]Improved category management:[/b] Manage display names, multiple parent categories, and SEO fields from one interface.
[*][b]Flexible workflow:[/b] Return to the original OpenCart lists or use the full product form whenever needed.
[/list]

[size=4][b]How It Works[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]The standard product and category lists are replaced with an AJAX-based management interface.
[*]Click any row to open its editor in a convenient side drawer.
[*]Edit the information you need and save. Only modified tabs are submitted.
[*]Data is saved through standard OpenCart models, while existing user permissions remain in effect.
[*]Disable the module or access the classic lists to restore the original management interface.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Important Notes & Limitations[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Third-party extension fields:[/b] Data stored outside standard OpenCart product fields may be reset when saving a product through the drawer. For products using such extensions, use the Full Product Form instead.
[*][b]Recurring tab:[/b] The Recurring tab is not currently included in the side-drawer editor.
[*][b]Required fields:[/b] A product must have a name and meta title in every active language before it can be saved.
[*][b]Category browser:[/b] The default category tree browser is replaced by a searchable interface.
[*][b]Language:[/b] Only the English (en-gb) language file is included.
[/list]

[list=1]

[size=4][b]Frequently Asked Questions[/b][/size]

[b]Does Quick Product Manager modify my existing products automatically?[/b]

No. Existing product data is only modified when you explicitly save an edit or perform an action, such as changing status, executing a bulk action, or using Quick Add.

[b]Can I still use the original OpenCart product form?[/b]

Yes. Use the Full Product Form button to access the standard OpenCart product editor. You can also access the original product and category lists through the classic list options.

[b]What happens when I uninstall the extension?[/b]

Your existing product and category data remain unchanged. The Display Name database table is retained.

[b]Does it support multiple languages and stores?[/b]

Yes. Product descriptions, SEO URLs, and layouts support multiple languages and stores.
